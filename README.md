With the development of technology, e-commerce has become a significant problem for online retailers and consumers. 
Criminals may use a variety of tactics to obtain goods or services from online merchants with the intention of not 
paying which causes major financial losses and damage to the business’s reputation.
**Project Goals:**
- Detect fraudulent transactions
- Developed using 4 algorithms: Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and XGBoost.

The dataset was retrieved from Kaggle.com. 
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/umuttuygurr/e-commerce-fraud-detection-dataset
